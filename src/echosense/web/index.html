PROVIDERS & PRIVACY

Connect services. Keep one intelligence.

Apple Music, Spotify, and future providers remain content and playback pipes. EchoSense normalizes permitted metadata, remembers preferences independently, and sends the selected track back to the preferred provider for playback.

1 · ConnectAuthorize one or more supported music services.
2 · NormalizeConvert permitted library, playlist, and feedback metadata into a provider-neutral taste profile.
3 · DecideUse context, memory, novelty, and safety to select a track and explain why.
4 · PlayResolve the track in the preferred connected service; the streaming provider delivers the audio.
